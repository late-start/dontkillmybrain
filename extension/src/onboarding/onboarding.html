dontkillmybrain

Are you thinking, or is AI thinking for you?

This extension quietly monitors your AI conversations and shows you how you're engaging cognitively. It helps you stay aware of when you're thinking critically versus passively accepting what AI gives you.

The Ambient Indicator

A small colored badge on the extension icon tells you how you're doing in real time:

Green — Engaged

You're steering the conversation, asking specific questions, pushing back, and contributing your own ideas.

Amber — Coasting

You're following along and making additions, but not deeply directing the conversation.

Red — Passive

You're mostly accepting AI output without critical evaluation. Short replies like "ok" or "sounds good" dominate.

Setup (Optional)

The extension works out of the box with local heuristics. For deeper AI-powered analysis of your conversations, you can connect it to the dontkillmybrain web app.
